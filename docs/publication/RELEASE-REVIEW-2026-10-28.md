## Restrictions retained in the manuscript

The short matched market window contains only 8.1 nominal 30-day blocks. Its
moving-block percentile intervals have unvalidated finite-sample coverage and
measurable edge weighting. The original intervals are retained with the new
diagnostic, rather than replaced by a favorable post-outcome estimator.

No candle-data analysis calibrates the synthetic shared stationary mean-cost
envelope for actual trading. Market costs remain assumptions; no latency,
capacity or authenticated-fill performance is inferred. The daily SMA extension
uses inspected history and unadjusted intervals; matched risk targets do not
equalize realized risk or establish causal signal alpha.
